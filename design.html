hi, i'm sara

Graphic Design

I am a graphic designer in Carroll, Iowa. Since 2024 I have taken on freelance clients alongside my marketing work: logos and brand identities, print design, and campaign materials. My rule is simple. Design has to earn its keep in the real world, on a sign, on a shelf, on a door someone drives past at forty miles an hour.

what i do

Logos and brand identity

A logo, the colors and type that go with it, and a short guide so everyone who touches it uses it the same way. Built to survive being embroidered, printed at an inch wide, and painted on a wall.

Print design

Brochures, menus, flyers, postcards, magnets, signage, billboards, event materials, stickers, and apparel graphics. You get press ready files, and I will talk to your printer directly so nothing gets lost between us.

Campaigns and digital

Websites, social content, and full material sets for political campaigns and causes: logos, mail, signs, and graphics that all look like they came from the same place, because they did.

The work

Identities I have built that are out in the world right now, most of them within a short drive of my house.

WESCO Industries. Identity for a nonprofit serving about 200 people across western Iowa, and the parent brand every program below answers to.

The Bake Shop and Cafe. Brand and menus for a working cafe where the bakers are the story.

Back 30 Event Center. Identity for an event hall with vintage bones and a dance floor.

Next Step Living. Identity for independent living with remote supports, roommates included.

NOVA. Identity for adult day services in Denison, built around what the letters actually promise: Nurture, Opportunity, Value, Accessibility.

The WAVE. Identity for a day habilitation program that needed energy, not clip art.

5th Avenue Fun Center. Identity for the home of WESCO's respite program, loud in the right ways, because a break should be fun.

Stepping Stones. Identity for transitional living and recovery support, calm on purpose.

THRIVE. Identity for a recognition program that celebrates the people doing the work.

Life. It's what we do.

LIFE. A full campaign identity for WESCO, eight programs, one promise, cereal boxes and all.

On shirts

How it works

One. You tell me what you are building and who it has to convince.

Two. I design concepts. You react honestly. We refine the one that is right.

Three. You get press ready and web ready files, in every format you will ever be asked for. The logo is yours, outright.

Every project is quoted flat before work begins. No hourly meter running, no surprise invoice.
